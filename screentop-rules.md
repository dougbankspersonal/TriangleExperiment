# Pyramid of Influence

# Terminology

- A tile is a trianglular playing piece with 4 sectors.
- Each sector is one of four neighborhood types: swamp, city, suburb, or farm.
- Mountain, meadow, and sand sectors have a symbol indicating which player they belong to.
- Swamp sectors do not have symbols.
- A set of adjacent sectors of the same neighborhood type forms a region of that type.
  - Adjacent means they share an edge: corners do not count.

# Setup

Shuffle the Bonus cards and place 3 face up.

Each player:

- Arranges their scoring cards face up in front of them.
- Shuffles their deck of tiles and draws one.

# Player Turn

Play a tile, then draw a new tile.
Playing a tile may result in Tokens being acquired, which may in turn trigger a scoring event.

## Rules for Placement

- New tile must be completely over or under played tiles.
- At least one sector of the new tile must be over or under a sector already played.
- At least one sector of the new tile must be neither over nor under a sector already played.
- If the new tile is over played tiles:
  - A swamp sector can cover any sector.
  - Any sector can cover a swamp sector.
  - Otherwise, the covered sector must match the color or symbol of the covering sector.

## Token Collection and Allocation.

When a tile is played _under_ other tiles, the active player collects one token for each of his symbols on the tile which is covered by the existing tiles.

When a tile is played _over_ other tiles, for each player symbol covered by the new tile, that player collects a token.

When a player collects a token they must immediately place it on one of their own active scoring cards.

# Scoring

## Triggering a Scoring Card

When tokens are added to a scoring card, if the card has 3 tokens, it is triggered.

## Bonus Card

The player whose scoring card was triggered may collect one of the face up Bonus cards. Replace it with a new Bonus card from the deck.

## Score

Score region(s) according to the rules on the scoring card.

### Scoring a region

- Determine which player(s) are **Dominant**: a player is **Dominant** if no other player has more symbols in the region (more than one player can be **Dominant** in a region)
- Each **Dominant** player scores one point for every opposing symbol in the region.
- Region scores should be kept separate by neighborhood type: a city region generates city points, etc.

## Cleanup

Return tokens from the card to the supply.
Flip and rotate the triggered card so it's clear it has been used.

# Bonus cards

A bonus card gives some perk which may be use one time: used Bonus cards should be set aside.

# Game End and Final Scoring

The game ends when either all tiles have been played, or a total of (3 + number of players) scoring cards have been triggered.

A player's final score is the _lowest_ of the 3 different neighborhood type scores.
